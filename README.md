## 💡 Job Search Tips for Lahore
* **LinkedIn is Key:** Most recruiters in Lahore are highly active on LinkedIn. Ensure your profile is professional and updated.
* **Network First:** Attend tech meetups in Arfa Software Technology Park or Plan9 to meet industry insiders.
* **Referrals:** Referrals are huge in the Pakistan tech scene. Try to connect with employees at your target companies before applying.
